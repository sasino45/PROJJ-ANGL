Official Tournament Rules

1. Helmet & Gear: Full closed helmet and secured footware are mandatory on the 700m track at all times.

2. Bumper Contact: Aggressive deliberate crashing or rear-ending will result in immediate black flag disqualification.

3. Flags System: • Yellow Flag: Hazard ahead, slow down, no overtaking. • Red Flag: Session stopped immediately, return to pits. • Checkered Flag: Finish line crossed.

4. Student Eligibility: Student drivers must present a valid student card (e.g. ENSA Tetouan) during desk verification.
